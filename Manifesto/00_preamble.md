By acknowledging the limits of the observer’s sensory reach, we allow the Manifold Engine to focus its full power on the immediate vicinity, creating a "bubble of reality" that is spatially honest, lexically defined, and computationally sustainable. We move from the "Dead-End" of static files to the "Living Threshold" of the morphological coordinate.
