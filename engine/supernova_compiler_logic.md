# Supernova Compiler Logic
**Role:** Automated Elemental Synthesis

## 1. Transition Trigger
When the `exponential_instancing` reaches critical mass density ($\rho > \rho_{crit}$), the engine switches from "Hydrogen State" to "Stellar Forge State."

## 2. The Compiler Sequence
1. **Fusion Cycle:** Hydrogen -> Helium -> Carbon -> Iron.
2. **Expansion/Collapse:** Upon Iron-core failure, the engine initiates the Supernova event.
3. **Synthesis:** Kinetic energy is used to "mint" the remaining 118 elements.
4. **Distribution:** The resulting elements are auto-populated as sub-tokens into the LVT's internal node surface.
